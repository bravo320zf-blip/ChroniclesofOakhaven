Rules of Oakhaven

Hub & Adventures: Between quests, rest and prepare in Oakhaven. Talk to patrons at the Rusty Flagon Tavern, Temple, and Market—or scout the Outskirts—to discover rumors of hidden adventures across the frontier.

Stash & Death Penalty: Dying in an adventure resurrects you at the Temple of the Dawn, but you lose all carried Gold, equipped Gear, and Backpack items! Always store spare gear and gold in your 20-Slot Stash & Gold Vault before dangerous quests.

Dice & Combat: Skill Checks roll 2d6 + Attribute (+2 if Skilled) ≥ DC. Test Your Luck rolls 2d6 ≤ Current Luck (reducing Luck by 1 until you rest at the Inn or Temple). Combat rolls 2d6 + Attack ≥ Enemy AC.

Progression & Crafting: Earn XP to level up (+4 Max HP, +2 Max MP, +1 Stat Point). At the Guildhall, each Attribute can be upgraded up to 3 times (500 / 1000 / 1500 GP), and you may learn up to 3 extra Skills (500 GP each) and 2 extra Spells (500 GP each).

Prepared Spells & Spell Scrolls: You may prepare up to 4 spells at a time while in Oakhaven. Only prepared spells can be cast while adventuring! Additional spells can be discovered as Rare Spell Scrolls in dungeon loot and learned from your Backpack.
